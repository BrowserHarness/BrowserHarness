# BrowserCrew MVP Terms

BrowserCrew v0.1 is an early-stage browser automation product.

## User control
Users remain responsible for the goals they provide, the websites they access, the accounts they use, and consequential actions they approve.

## AI output
AI model output can be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable. BrowserCrew verifies browser mutations where possible, but users should review important results.

## Website rules
Users are responsible for complying with website terms, applicable laws, and account policies when using BrowserCrew.

## Consequential actions
BrowserCrew requires approval for identified send, submit, purchase, delete, payment, and account/security-changing actions. This safeguard does not guarantee that every website-specific risk can be identified automatically.

## Provider accounts
Users supply their own AI provider credentials. Provider availability, limits, pricing, data handling, and service terms are controlled by those providers.

## Availability
The MVP is provided for testing and early use. Features and interfaces may change as the product is hardened.

## Prohibited secret sharing
Users should not intentionally place passwords, private keys, authentication cookies, or other secrets into BrowserCrew prompts or recorded workflows.

## Future updates
Commercial release terms may replace this MVP document before public paid distribution.
